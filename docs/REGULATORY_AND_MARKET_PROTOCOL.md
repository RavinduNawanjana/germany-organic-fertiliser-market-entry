# Germany product-market evidence: law, classification and costs

## A two-path market screen, never an automatic CE compliance decision

The original market-entry outline discusses EU Fertilising Products Regulation **Regulation (EU) 2019/1009**, PFC/CMC classification, documentation, contaminants, labelling, distribution, organic-sector requirements and competitors. The primary legal text is [EUR-Lex Regulation 2019/1009](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1009/oj). **Crucial clarification:** the EU-harmonised CE route does not abolish national fertiliser regimes. Products may instead be supplied under applicable national law and mutual-recognition rules where conditions are met. See the [European Commission description](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/LSU/?uri=CELEX%3A32019R1009). The current route, product formulation and classification are **unknown**.

Evidence requests are grouped by market route, PFC, CMC, exact ingredients/feedstocks, nutrient laboratory reports, contaminant and microbiology testing, German-language labels and instructions, assessment route, importer responsibilities, national rules, and organic-use eligibility. FiBL-related listing may matter to target buyers but is **not a blanket legal requirement for all fertiliser sales nor equivalent to product regulatory compliance**; obtain scheme-specific advice.

The `screen_evidence()` function can say *ready for external specialist assessment* when each row has documented review, but **never declares legal conformity**. Actual product chemistry, legal duty code, pesticide/biostimulant borderlines, organic production permissions, local application restrictions and importer status require qualified legal/technical review.

## Illustrative landed unit economics

Modelled purchase cost `Q × production_cost_USD/kg × EUR_per_USD`; plus assumed freight + insurance; a **toy customs duty rate** on an assumed simplified CIF proxy; plus port, testing and warehousing costs. `unit landed = summed cost / Q`; contribution `selling_price − landed_unit`; operating result `Q × contribution − fixed_market_launch`; one-shipment break-even quantity `(fixed_market_launch + fixed_shipment/handling/testing) / (selling_price − true variable_cost_per_kg)` only where incremental contribution is positive; it is **not** calculated by dividing only launch cost by a contribution that already amortises fixed shipment fees. No VAT recovery, financing, EU tariff classification, HS/CN origin preferences or real customer willingness-to-pay is established. No number is a current freight, FX, customs or wholesale quotation.
