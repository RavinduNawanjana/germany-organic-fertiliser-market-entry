# Research programme and unanswered questions

## Research object
Route-specific PFC/CMC evidence register, unit economics, landed-cost sensitivity and decision gates.

## Evidence needed to move beyond screening
- Primary operational observations, with documented units, dates, boundaries and consent.
- Authoritative factor databases or measurement methods with version and geography.
- Independent QA and inclusion/exclusion rules documented before quantitative comparison.
- Version-controlled project files; evidence chain from source to reported outcome.
- Robust uncertainty analysis based on empirically defensible distributions, not arbitrary pilot settings.
- Independent replication against a second method or externally verified benchmark, when available.

## Explicit current limitation
FPR versus national marketing routes, product chemistry, importer responsibilities and current pricing require confirmation.

## Next steps for a publishable working paper
1. Freeze a transparent analysis plan, parameter provenance and hypotheses.
2. Replace labelled illustrative examples with consent-cleared, verified observations.
3. Quantify uncertainty consistent with data quality and observational design.
4. Invite external code review and substantive domain-methodology review.
5. Distinguish research inferences from operational recommendations, approvals and implementation claims.
